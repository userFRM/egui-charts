title: Chart types and series
---

[`ChartType`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/model/enum.ChartType.html) selects the main series renderer. The available types include
candlesticks, OHLC bars, line and area charts, Heikin Ashi, Renko, Kagi,
Point & Figure, Line Break, range bars, Market Profile (TPO), Volume Footprint and Session Volume Profile.

The two execution chart types use retained and incoming feed trades on time-based intervals and require the instrument's tick size. Without those inputs they draw candles, as do bars before the first bar whose executions are all retained; the bar of the oldest retained trade counts only once the feed reports that no older trades exist. Session Volume Profile also requires the `studies` feature.

```rust
use egui_charts::{Chart, ChartType, model::BarData};

let mut chart = Chart::new(BarData::new());
chart.set_chart_type(ChartType::Line);
```

Price transforms use the supplied history and the chart type's parameters. Choose
parameters with [`Chart::set_chart_type_params`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.set_chart_type_params); use the
[API reference](api-reference.mdx) for [`ChartTypeParams`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/model/struct.ChartTypeParams.html) and each transform's definition.
Execution-based intervals and price-transform renderers are separate concepts:
selecting a renderer does not acquire trades or replace the feed's interval.

## Add a series

[`Chart::add_series`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.add_series) adds another series; retain its returned [`SeriesId`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/chart/series/selection/struct.SeriesId.html) for updates
and removal. [`SeriesOptions`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.SeriesOptions.html) controls its appearance and scale. Update each series
through its own id, and use [`SeriesScale`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/model/enum.SeriesScale.html) to select shared or independent pricing.
The main series remains [`SeriesId::MAIN`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/chart/series/selection/struct.SeriesId.html#associatedconstant.MAIN).

[Scales and navigation](scales.mdx) explains coordinate sharing. The repository's
[basic chart example](https://github.com/userFRM/egui-charts/blob/main/examples/basic_chart.rs)
shows data, chart events and an additional price line.
