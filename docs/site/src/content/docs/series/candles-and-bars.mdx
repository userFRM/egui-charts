Use OHLC when the open, high, low and close within each interval matter. The
[series overview](../series.mdx) explains how to add, update, hide and remove a
series; the [input table](../chart-types.mdx#inputs-for-every-main-chart-type)
covers every renderer.

## Data

[`Bar::new(time, open, high, low, close, volume)`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/model/bar/struct.Bar.html#method.new)
stores one interval at its UTC open time. High must be at least the open and
close; low must be at most both. Volume must be finite and nonnegative; use zero
if unavailable. For example, `(100, 105, 98, 103, 800)` has a body from 100 to
103, a wick from 98 to 105 and rising colour under the default open/close rule.

[`ChartType`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/model/enum.ChartType.html)
offers `Candles`, `Bars`, `HollowCandles`, `VolumeCandles` and `HighLow`.
Bars draw an open tick on the left and a close tick on the right. Hollow candles
are hollow when close is above open. Volume candles scale body width against the
largest visible bar volume; zero volume gives a body as thin as the wick.
High-low draws the interval's range as one bar in a single colour, whatever the
direction, and prints each bar's high and low beyond its ends once the bars are
wide enough for every label.

The compiled setup is in [series.rs](https://github.com/userFRM/egui-charts/blob/main/examples/docs/scenes/series.rs).
[`ChartTypeStyles::candles`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/model/struct.ChartTypeStyles.html#structfield.candles)
holds [`CandlesStyle`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/model/struct.CandlesStyle.html):
body visibility, wick visibility and colouring by previous close.
[`BarsStyle`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/model/struct.BarsStyle.html)
controls the open tick, thin bars and colouring by previous close.
[`HighLowStyle`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/model/struct.HighLowStyle.html)
sets the high-low bar colour and the visibility, colour and size of its price
labels, which do not read the candle colours. The label colour is `Color32::PLACEHOLDER` by
default, which prints the labels in the price scale's text colour, and the size is drawn at 1 to 256
points. Apply these through
[`Chart::set_chart_type_params`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.set_chart_type_params).

Main-series colours, wick width and candle border width live in
[`ChartConfig`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/config/struct.ChartConfig.html#structfield.bullish_color).
[`BarColors`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/model/bar/struct.BarColors.html)
can override a bar's body, wick and border. Added OHLC series use
[`SeriesOptions::up_color`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.SeriesOptions.html#structfield.up_color)
and `down_color`, and draw each bar's border colour inside its body when
[`SeriesOptions::candle_border_width`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.SeriesOptions.html#structfield.candle_border_width)
is positive (zero by default). Added Bars series take `bars_style` for the open
tick, thin bars and colouring by previous close; they do not expose the main series' candle style switches.
Added high-low series draw in
[`SeriesOptions::color`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.SeriesOptions.html#structfield.color)
and tag their last value and draw their price line in it, without price labels. All five types can be [added series](../chart-types.mdx#added-series-restrictions).

For smoothed OHLC, continue to [Heikin-Ashi](/egui-charts/docs/chart-types/transforms/#heikin-ashi).
