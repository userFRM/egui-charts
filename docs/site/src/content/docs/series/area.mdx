An area plot fills between a value line and a pane edge. The
[series overview](../series.mdx) covers data updates and removal.

## Data

[`ChartType::Area`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/model/enum.ChartType.html#variant.Area)
accepts a scalar via
[`Bar::from_value`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/model/bar/struct.Bar.html#method.from_value)
or OHLC bars. The main series reads
[`ChartConfig::price_source`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/config/struct.ChartConfig.html#structfield.price_source),
close by default; added areas read close. Whitespace breaks the line and fill.

[`ChartType::HlcArea`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/model/enum.ChartType.html#variant.HlcArea)
needs high, low and close in a valid OHLC bar. It fills the high-to-close and
close-to-low bands rather than filling to the pane edge.

## Style and running result

This scene fills below the sample close line, fading to transparency at the
bottom. It uses the [shared synthetic OHLC history](../first-chart.mdx).

The compiled setup is in [series.rs](https://github.com/userFRM/egui-charts/blob/main/examples/docs/scenes/series.rs).
[`AreaStyle`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/model/struct.AreaStyle.html)
sets the edge line, top and bottom fill colours, gradient coordinates and fill
direction. `relative_gradient` defaults to true, spreading the gradient from the
visible line extreme to the pane edge; false uses the whole pane.
`invert_filled_area` fills upward. Apply it through
[`ChartTypeStyles::area`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/model/struct.ChartTypeStyles.html#structfield.area).

For HLC area,
[`HlcAreaStyle`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/model/struct.HlcAreaStyle.html)
sets the colour, width and visibility of the high and low lines, the close line,
and the colour, opacity and visibility of each band. Added areas use
[`SeriesOptions::color`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.SeriesOptions.html#structfield.color)
and `width` with the default downward gradient, unless `area_style` supplies the
line and gradient settings above. Added HLC areas take their high line and upper
band from `up_color` and their low line and lower band from `down_color`. Both types can be
[added series](../chart-types.mdx#added-series-restrictions).

For fills on both sides of a reference, use [baseline](/egui-charts/docs/series/baseline/).
