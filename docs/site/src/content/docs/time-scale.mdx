The time scale places bars horizontally. Logical coordinates address this
chart's slots; UTC coordinates address instants that other charts can share.
The scene uses the [first chart's](first-chart.mdx) 120 fixed one-minute bars.

## Try fitting, scrolling and ranges

Use **Fit history**, **Back 30 bars**, **Live edge** and **Reset time**. Compare
**Logical 20..59** with **UTC minutes 20..59**, then select **Future space** to
extend past the last bar. Readouts describe the last layout; they also update
when you pan or zoom directly.
Call the scene's `show(ui, &mut chart)` before `chart.show(ui)` on each frame.

## Fit and scroll

[`fit_content`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.fit_content)
fits the stored history of the main series and of every added series, hidden ones included,
and keeps the configured right margin.
[`scroll_to_position`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.scroll_to_position)
positions the latest bar relative to the right edge in bars: positive positions
leave space on the right, negative positions scroll into history.
[`scroll_to_real_time`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.scroll_to_real_time)
returns to the live edge while keeping zoom; it is a one-time scroll. Its
`animated` flag, also used by `scroll_to_position`, animates the move. These
scenes use fixed data; a host supplies historical and streaming updates.

[`reset_time_scale`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.reset_time_scale)
restores the configured spacing and right offset. It leaves the price ranges
alone. [`reset_chart_view`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.reset_chart_view)
also returns every pane's price scales to autoscale.

## Logical indices and UTC instants

[`visible_logical_range`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.visible_logical_range)
returns a [`LogicalRange`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/model/struct.LogicalRange.html).
Index 0 is the oldest slot in this chart; bounds can be fractional or outside
the data. A range spans `right - left + 1` bar widths, so `20..59` spans 40.
[`set_visible_logical_range`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.set_visible_logical_range)
sets spacing and scroll together. It returns `false` for empty data, non-finite
bounds or reversed bounds, keeping the view unchanged. Spacing and edge limits
still apply. Logical indices depend on the chart's history and interval; do not
copy them between charts with different slots.

[`visible_time_range`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.visible_time_range)
returns a [`VisibleTimeRange`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.VisibleTimeRange.html)
in UTC, including whitespace. Use
[`apply_synced_time_range`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.apply_synced_time_range)
to fit those instants to another chart's width. Check its boolean result:
empty data, invalid or wholly disjoint ranges, and endpoints mapping to one
element are rejected. Its maximum spacing is the configured limit, or 50 points
when unlimited, so very short spans may be centred rather than filling the plot.

[`index_to_time`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.index_to_time)
and [`time_to_index`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.time_to_index)
convert between fractional slots and UTC. Times between bars are interpolated;
outside the history they are extrapolated using the interval or observed edge
spacing. Calendar month and year intervals follow calendar stepping, as detailed
in the method documentation. Display time zones affect labels, while the data
and shared time ranges remain UTC.

## Leave future whitespace

The last sample bar has logical index 119. The scene's `80..139` range leaves
20 logical slots beyond it without inventing future OHLC values. To include a
known empty timestamp in supplied history, use
[`Bar::whitespace`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/model/bar/struct.Bar.html#method.whitespace).

[`TimeScaleOptions`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/config/struct.TimeScaleOptions.html)
is available through
[`ChartOptions::time_scale`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/config/struct.ChartOptions.html#structfield.time_scale).
Edit a copy from `chart_options()` and apply it with
[`set_chart_options`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.set_chart_options).

- [`right_offset`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/config/struct.TimeScaleOptions.html#structfield.right_offset)
  sets live-edge space in bars;
  [`right_offset_pixels`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/config/struct.TimeScaleOptions.html#structfield.right_offset_pixels)
  takes priority when present.
- [`bar_spacing`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/config/struct.TimeScaleOptions.html#structfield.bar_spacing),
  [`min_bar_spacing`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/config/struct.TimeScaleOptions.html#structfield.min_bar_spacing)
  and [`max_bar_spacing`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/config/struct.TimeScaleOptions.html#structfield.max_bar_spacing)
  control zoom and constrain fitting.
- [`fix_left_edge`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/config/struct.TimeScaleOptions.html#structfield.fix_left_edge)
  and [`fix_right_edge`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/config/struct.TimeScaleOptions.html#structfield.fix_right_edge)
  constrain scrolling, including requested whitespace.
- [`lock_visible_time_range_on_resize`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/config/struct.TimeScaleOptions.html#structfield.lock_visible_time_range_on_resize)
  keeps the visible time range when the chart size changes.

## Wait for plot geometry

Call [`Chart::show`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.show)
once at the intended size before fitting a range or converting screen positions.
The range setters use the **last drawn plot width**; after resizing, show again
before applying a width-dependent range. The scene enables its buttons once
[`time_scale_size`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.time_scale_size)
reports a positive width.

[`index_to_x`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.index_to_x)
and [`x_to_index`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.x_to_index)
use screen points in the chart's egui `Ui`, with `x` at the bar centre. They
return `None` before the first layout. For separate charts, choose one source
and forward only its changed UTC range and
[`hover_time`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.hover_time)
to [`set_synced_crosshair_time`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.set_synced_crosshair_time).
The [builder guide](../../../../builder-guide.md#synchronize-charts) and
[synchronized charts example](https://github.com/userFRM/egui-charts/blob/main/examples/synchronized_charts.rs)
show that integration.
