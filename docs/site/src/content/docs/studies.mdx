Studies compute values from the bars your application supplies. Create them
once, then keep the chart across frames so it can update their values when
history arrives or the current bar changes.

Enable `studies` for the built-in catalogue and [`IndicatorFactory`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/studies/factory/struct.IndicatorFactory.html). The [`Indicator`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/studies/trait.Indicator.html)
trait, [`IndicatorValue`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/studies/enum.IndicatorValue.html), [`CustomIndicator`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/studies/struct.CustomIndicator.html) and [`IndicatorRegistry`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/studies/struct.IndicatorRegistry.html) are also available
in the headless engine.

## Attach a built-in study

This scene adds an [`EMA`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/studies/struct.EMA.html)
with a period of 20 and an [`RSI`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/studies/struct.RSI.html)
with a period of 14 to the [first chart's](first-chart.mdx) fixed one-minute bars.
The EMA follows price in the main pane. RSI has its own pane and scale below
price; both panes share time navigation and the crosshair. Drag their separator
to resize them. Volume is hidden to make the study placement easier to see.

[`Chart::add_indicator`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.add_indicator)
calculates the study immediately and returns a
[`SeriesId`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/chart/series/selection/struct.SeriesId.html).
Retain that id for
[`set_indicator_visible`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.set_indicator_visible),
[`indicator_mut`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.indicator_mut)
or [`remove_series`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.remove_series).
Do not add a study on every frame. Call
[`Chart::show`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.show)
on the retained chart instead.

The chart recalculates after source bars change and places the study according
to [`Indicator::is_overlay`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/studies/trait.Indicator.html#method.is_overlay).
The EMA seeds from the first close and smooths with `2 / (period + 1)`;
it has no missing warm-up values. RSI needs 14 price changes before its first
value and uses Wilder's smoothing. Neither needs a second feed. See
[pane placement and resizing](panes.mdx) and the [built-in catalogue](api-reference.mdx).

## Write a custom study

[`CustomIndicator`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/studies/struct.CustomIndicator.html) accepts a `Send + Sync` calculation closure. Return one value
per source bar and use [`IndicatorValue::None`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/studies/enum.IndicatorValue.html#variant.None) during warm-up or when input is
unavailable. Try [a custom study with warm-up and gaps](/egui-charts/docs/studies/custom/)
to see that alignment in a running chart. The [builder guide](../../../../builder-guide.md#custom-studies)
also includes a one-bar-change example. Implement [`Indicator`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/studies/trait.Indicator.html) directly for incremental
calculation; [`studies::helpers`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/studies/helpers/index.html) and [`Stepper`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/studies/struct.Stepper.html) support reusable scalar operations.

## Load history and update a forming bar

The [live data scene](/egui-charts/docs/studies/live-data/) answers real history
requests from an in-memory sample and feeds synthetic ticks from elapsed time.
Try loading older bars, then return to the latest bar and watch its candle and
EMA update. Your application supplies the historical and streaming data in
production; the library manages their chart state and study recalculation.

## Supply additional data

Some studies need another interval, another symbol, instrument metrics or actual
executions. A connected feed loads declared study data; direct hosts answer
[`Chart::study_data_requests`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.study_data_requests) with [`set_study_data`](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/api/egui_charts/widget/struct.Chart.html#method.set_study_data). Read
[calendar and viewport studies](../../../../study-data.md) for intrabar requirements and missing
values, and [order flow](../../../../order-flow.md) for event-driven inputs.

The host may evaluate scripts and expose their results through a custom study.
The library does not provide a scripting runtime.
