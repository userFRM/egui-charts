# egui-charts Roadmap

[Feature status and progress](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/progress/) distinguishes independently verified, unverified, incomplete and missing behaviour. Checked items below describe implementation; the status page records independent verification.

The crate provides an engine without egui, an egui 0.37 chart widget, and terminal UI components. Checked items describe working capabilities, including public APIs that applications can compose. Unchecked items describe remaining library work. `ui::terminal::Terminal` assembles the components on the host's feed and services and lets the application intercept their actions; it returns those it does not perform yet, and the demo still routes the components by hand.

## Chart Rendering Engine

### Chart Types

`ChartType` offers 21 chart types, with the rendering limits listed below.

- [x] Candles, OHLC bars, hollow candles and volume candles, with configurable rising and falling colours. Hollow and volume candles keep independent body, border and wick visibility and rising/falling colours, which persist in layouts and undo; volume candles and OHLC bars can also compare each close with the previous close. Added candle series accept a border width and per-bar body, wick and border colours; borders stay inside the body and default to disabled. The last price line, its label and `series_last_value` of columns, hollow candles, and candles and volume candles coloured by the previous close, take the direction of the last candle. Hollow candles also take a bar's own body, border and wick colours.
- [x] Line, line with markers, step line, area, HLC area, baseline, columns and high-low charts; line and area charts accept a `PriceSource`, and the Columns chart keeps its own for the price each column reaches. High-low charts draw every bar in one colour and print each bar's high and low once zoomed in far enough to fit them, with independent label visibility, colour and size that persist in layouts and undo. Added column series apply their configured hit tolerance around their painted bounds, including flat bodies, dense bars and single-point histories; zero uses the body bounds and all hits stay inside the plot. Baseline fills accept independent colours at the pane edge and the base on each price side, including point overrides, while older settings keep flat fills. Area and baseline plots support point markers with independent radii for main and added series. Added series retain their own curved and patterned lines, OHLC open-tick visibility and thin bars, area gradient endpoints, relative gradients and fill inversion. Line-based added series pulse continuously or on last-bar updates on their own pane and scale; expired update pulses stop requesting repaints.
- [x] Main-series conflation has a configurable physical-pixel threshold or can be disabled. `Chart::prepare_conflation` prepares the shared render cache before a dense view; groups retain price extremes, gaps and per-bar colours and refresh after data, price-source or grouping changes.
- [x] Heikin-Ashi, Renko, Kagi, line break, point and figure, and range-bar transformations and rendering. Heikin-Ashi retains independent body, border and wick visibility and rising/falling colours; Symbol settings also select original-only scale prices or the default pair of original and transformed closes. These choices persist in layouts and undo.
- [x] Renko box sizing and wicks, line-break lookback, and price-based chart parameters are configurable.
- [x] Market Profile (TPO) uses exchange sessions or calendar days, with letters or blocks, initial balance, point of control, value area and composite profiles; autoscale fits the full displayed distributions and cropped profiles retain their session and central-level readouts. POC, value-area and optional midpoint captions keep a gutter at the first or offscreen session opening, separate nearby levels and reveal their prices on hover.
- [x] Volume Footprint and Session Volume Profile aggregate supplied executions on time, tick, volume, range and delta intervals with an instrument tick size; bars without complete retained executions use candles. Session profiles require `studies`, divide the chart by the chart's hours, the instrument's regular or extended hours, or a daily window of their own in any zone, and group executions into a row count or whole ticks per row; autoscale includes their full price rows after range edits, and manual crops retain session and POC/value-area readouts. Hosts can restrict the chart type menu and favourite strip to declared historical or live executions for the resolved instrument; unavailable favourites stay saved.
- [ ] Leave bars outside the chosen session hours out of session volume profiles instead of joining them to the session before.
- [ ] Change Kagi line thickness at shoulder and waist crossings within a continuing leg.
- [ ] Expose the standalone point-and-figure close-only source in chart parameters and settings.
- [x] Range charts build their bars from the supplied executions, falling back to the OHLC approximation while the chart holds none; `Chart::range_bar_source` reports which one is shown.
- [ ] Offer proportional volume-candle widths without the current minimum width.
- [x] HLC area charts draw high, low and close lines with their own colour, width and style, and two fills with their own colour and opacity; high line, low line and each fill can be hidden. The settings persist in layouts and undo. Kagi stroke widths are exposed in the settings.
- [x] TPO single-print highlights and the midpoint caption and marker are off by default and switch on from the chart's settings or the study's inputs.

### Pan, Zoom and Cursor

- [x] Cursor-centred wheel zoom, horizontal pan, drag pan, touch pan and pinch zoom. Scroll and scale gesture groups can be disabled independently without changing their individual permissions; disabling scrolling stops its momentum, and the scale switch also covers axis drags and resets in every pane.
- [x] Touch drags can start kinetic scrolling with configurable damping.
- [x] Axis drag scaling, double-click reset, box zoom and optional chart-owned navigation buttons. Fit content includes every loaded bar series across panes, including hidden series and whitespace, reserves the configured bar or pixel right margin, and respects spacing limits. An empty main series uses the union of added-series timestamps for its horizontal mapping. Live updates preserve the view on valued bars; filling a whitespace tail follows only when both shift options are enabled and the view is near the live edge. Appended bars retain their new-bar follow policy. Bars arriving between frames, older or newer and in any number, on a chart type that rebuilds its bars or on a chart without main bars, keep a following view at its live edge, including with a fixed right edge, and a scrolled-back view on its bars.
- [x] Programmatic crosshair positions target a bar series in its own price units, scale and pane, with pointer precedence and matching image exports. Invalid positions and removed targets clear the crosshair.
- [x] Cross, dot and arrow cursor modes, styled crosshair lines, price/time labels and OHLC snapping. Both lines and both axis labels have independent visibility switches; hidden mode suppresses every crosshair element. Independent time-scale filters exclude whitespace and hidden-only values from crosshair indices without removing stored data; visible series and study values keep their slots eligible. Line-based main and added series accept independent crosshair marker radii, fills, border colours and border widths, including on independent scales and in separate panes. Markers start hidden; enabling them without colour overrides keeps the point's line colour and a chart-background ring.
- [x] Main-pane crosshair snapping includes visible studies and added series on their own scales for time-based charts. Close-only snapping compares visible main and added-series closes on every chart type, preferring the main series and then added-series draw order on equal screen distances.
- [x] Hover readouts and the data window show bar and study values. Touch holds activate after a configurable delay, move the readout without panning, and exit on release or retain their position until the next tap, independently of live-edge following.
- [ ] Allow mouse drags to start kinetic scrolling.
- [ ] Include added-series values in broader main-pane magnet snapping.
- [ ] Correct box-zoom overlay alpha and use theme colours.
- [ ] Add the instrument title to the floating historical readout.
- [ ] Provide a table view of loaded bars and study values.

### Scales and Panes

- [x] Linear, logarithmic, percentage, indexed-to-100 and inverted price scales, autoscale, manual ranges and scale options. Series autoscale providers and attached primitives can reserve independent top and bottom logical-pixel margins, combined by edge maximum within each pane and scale and added to fractional margins after resizing; manual ranges ignore them, a scale with no price to fit keeps its range, and a pane too short for a request keeps its ordinary fit, including the room for markers. Main and added series independently style their 0% or indexed-100 reference lines with colour, width, pattern and visibility; saved styles retain these settings. Marker autoscaling reserves room for shapes, outlines and labels of visible markers positioned from bars or prices at their configured size on main and secondary panes; hidden markers and disabled autoscaling add no room.
- [x] Time-axis tick hierarchy, price marks that default to the instrument tick grid with host overrides, exchange time zones, session breaks and extended-hours shading.
- [x] Linear numeric horizontal scales for strikes and maturities use actual sample keys without placeholder bars; series, panes, ticks and cursor conversions share their positions. Time charts retain ordinal spacing. Custom horizontal domains accept categorical and structured host keys ordered by their type, with shared formatting, typed lookup and annotation placement; unknown keys are rejected and repeated keys update one slot.
- [x] Chart-wide time, price, percentage and volume formatters, plus per-series price formatters. Saved series styles declare price, abbreviated volume or raw percentage display with precision and minimum movement, independently of scale transforms. Explicit series and chart price formatters take precedence; a shared axis takes tick settings from its first visible series, and each series retains its own tags and readouts. Price and percentage tick hooks receive the complete ordered list for each axis in its displayed units, including edge ticks, while crosshair and price tags keep individual-value formatting. Time tick hooks receive the current locale and display-time context and can defer individual ticks to the normal locale-aware text without changing crosshair formatting. Drawing prices and price changes use their pane's raw-price formatter, including measurements, forecasts, projections, position levels and rectangle heights.
- [x] Locale-aware date names and configurable date patterns. Forecast dates use the chart's display time zone and crosshair time formatter, including host formatting and clock visibility.
- [x] Chart-owned panes share the time axis and support resizing, reordering, stretch sizing, collapse and maximise controls. Ctrl/Command plus double-click collapses or restores the pointed pane, including its header, and preserves sizing, focus, undo and saved layouts; outside every pane it stays an ordinary double-click. With the price pane collapsed, the other panes fill the chart and keep dragging, scrolling and pinching the time scale. Removing any pane, including the original price pane, deletes its members and annotations while preserving surviving panes and their horizontal mapping; the final pane is kept. Snapshots preserve layouts without the original pane; the main series stays out of such a layout while its bars keep arriving from the host or the feed for the studies and the time axis.
- [x] Main and added series and studies can move between panes. The main series retains its data, horizontal mapping, price scale, annotations and pointer readouts in its destination; snapshots and undo preserve placement. Studies and added series can use separate panes and independent scales. Named left, right, additional and hidden overlay assignments share options and ranges only within their pane; painting, axis controls, hit tests and coordinate queries follow those assignments after pane moves, and snapshots retain each pane's named scales. The demo menu's Samples entries open synthetic strike and maturity charts with numeric crosshair readouts and preserve the price workspace on return.
- [x] Pane-local axis enumeration and commands place every labelled scale at either edge or join their sources onto one scale while retaining raw values and series formatting. Unused axes hide by default and return when a source becomes visible; the policy and layout survive saving and undo. Axis and plot menus expose these controls in the library and demo, and chart settings edit the main series' pane.
- [x] Scale settings control currency and unit labels, scale-mode and price-action buttons, price-to-bar ratio, label overlap and symbol, study and extended-session value colours. The demo menu converts synthetic USD prices to US cents through the host feed contract, including history, executions and book prices, and keeps the unit through replay and saved layouts.
- [ ] Forward pan, wheel and pinch input to maximised study panes and support pane maximisation by double-click.
- [ ] Apply the main-series-only autoscale quick setting and the left-axis scale-mode configuration field.
- [x] Added-series value tags use configured scale precision when no formatter is supplied; saved numeric display declarations also control their tags and readouts.
- [ ] Draw independent symbol-name and study-name tags on the price scale.
- [ ] Apply scale font-size, scale-line colour and pane-button visibility settings.

## Drawing Tools

### Geometry and Annotations

- [x] Trend lines, rays, extended lines, horizontal and vertical levels, cross lines, trend angle and info line. Trend lines, rays, extended lines, info lines and trend angles offer independent price, percentage, pip, bar, elapsed-time, length and inclination readouts, selection-only or persistent display, left/centre/right/automatic placement, anchor price labels, text alignment along and across the line and a movable midpoint; settings survive undo, templates and saves. Info lines write their readouts in their own text colour, opacity, size, weight and slant, keep them visible by default, and extend past the anchor each side names, whichever way they were drawn. Trend angles do the same, and set the font, weight, slant and colour of their angle text. Extended lines write their text in its own colour, opacity, size, weight and slant, and extend past the anchor each side names, whichever way they were drawn. Horizontal lines write their text in its own colour, opacity, size, weight and slant, aligned along the pane and above, on or below the line, interrupting the line when the text sits on it.

### Interaction, Properties and Persistence

- [x] Fixed-point placement keeps clicked anchors; free paths finish with Enter or double-click.
- [x] Selection uses rendered strokes, fills and text, with geometry and anchor fallbacks before painting. Ctrl/Command-drag on empty pane space adds drawings intersecting the selection area, including locked drawings for inspection; editing still honours locks. Escape cancels the area gesture.

Both magnet strengths include visible study plots on their rendered scales, in whichever pane holds the main series, and leave out the bars and volume of a hidden main series; disabling study snapping leaves bar and drawing targets available. The aggregate hide control follows the active chart and toggles supported groups together.
- [x] Undoable drawing creation, edits, deletion, ordering, flat groups and moves between panes. Bulk removal asks before including locked drawings, can remember either choice, and restores drawings and editable studies together in one undo step.
- [x] Properties edit style, tool inputs, text, timeframe visibility and validated price/time coordinates. Drawing menus and the selected toolbar offer current, higher, lower and all-interval visibility filters, with undo and saved templates. Time intervals compare by duration; trade intervals compare within their own unit.
- [x] Public timestamp-based creation and anchor editing, drawing enumeration, per-tool defaults and custom drawing registration. Creation uses each tool's style before saved overrides: arcs and polylines start with a 20% tint, price ranges and quick measure with a 15% tint, anchored VWAP at 1 px, and pitchfans with individual level colours. A pin's popup is a card in the theme's colours, tied to the pin by a thin line; a table draws its grid and border in the drawing colour; and the default colours of box borders and table grids take a shade that keeps 4:1 on the theme.
- [x] Per-drawing selection, saving and object-list flags; drawing change notifications and registry availability controls.
- [x] Versioned drawing documents retain timestamp anchors, settings and flat groups, with migration and skipped-record reporting.
- [x] `Chart::set_storage` loads and saves drawings per symbol and retains failed writes for retry.
- [x] Drawing templates save, apply and delete reusable styles through `TemplateStore` and `Storage`.
- [x] Demonstration cursor with a fading pointer trail.
- [x] Nested drawing folders in the object tree. New folders take the selected drawings and any fully selected folders. A folder row selects its descendants, drags into another folder or out to the root, and deletes with its contents in one undo step. Emptied folders go away and an undo restores them; cycles and name clashes are refused. A top-level folder keeps its drawings on the chart, mirrors them across the layout or follows the layout-wide switch. Nested folders follow it and keep their mode at the root. ## Order Flow

Execution-based calculations use supplied trades and their aggressor side. Unknown-side volume remains separate; chart-bar volume estimates do not reconstruct actual executions.

### Footprints, Delta and Profiles

- [x] `Footprint` aggregates bid, ask and unknown volume and execution counts by time or trade-based bar and integer tick row, with execution-size filtering against original fills. Grouped rows add their counts and average size divides total volume by executions, including unknown sides; empty rows and bars have no average. Volume and delta thresholds split fills exactly, counting a contributing execution once per bar, equal exchange timestamps retain separate bars, and interval changes rebuild from retained executions.
- [x] Footprint cells support a default ladder with the sell column left and the buy column right of a narrow candle body, plus bid/ask, total volume, delta, delta-plus-volume, sell/buy execution counts, total executions and average execution size modes, up to four columns per bar, profile or boxed rendering, automatic or fixed-tick row grouping and opening/closing price ticks in a separate gutter; their values have clearance from underlying price guides, and fixed grouped rows fit within autoscale. The statistics table offers Trades and Average size rows.
- [x] Per-bar point of control, value area and horizontal/diagonal imbalance highlighting, with one inclusive minimum dominant-side volume for cells and stacked zones after row grouping; compact values fit detail columns and POC outlines recede in the overview. By default sell and buy cells shade separately in four volume steps, the POC row is filled, the value area is two dotted lines, imbalanced sides get an edge strip, and each ladder bar writes its sell and buy totals with a delta and total volume box.
- [x] Public calculations and renderers expose stacked diagonal imbalance zones, unrevisited POC levels, volume clusters and per-bar statistics; annotation captions avoid footprint values, with compact overview marks and hover details. Both Symbol dialogs expose footprint display, rows, levels, imbalances, annotations, numbers and statistics, plus execution-profile display, value area, developing POC and volume nodes. These settings save, restore and undo with the chart; footprint colours follow the theme until selected.
- [x] Execution-based delta histograms and cumulative-delta candles, including intrabar extremes and configurable reset boundaries, render in a supplied pane mapping.
