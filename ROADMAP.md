# egui-charts Roadmap

[Feature status and progress](https://userfrm.github.io/egui-charts/docs/progress/) distinguishes independently verified, unverified, incomplete and missing behaviour. Checked items below describe implementation; the status page records independent verification.

The crate provides an engine without egui, an egui 0.37 chart widget, and terminal UI components. Checked items describe working capabilities, including public APIs that applications can compose. Unchecked items describe remaining library work. `egui_charts_terminal::Terminal`, in the unpublished `terminal` package, assembles the components on the host's feed and services and lets the application intercept their actions; it returns those it does not perform yet, and the demo still routes the components by hand.

## Chart Rendering Engine

### Chart Types

`ChartType` offers 21 chart types, with the rendering limits listed below.

- [x] Candles, OHLC bars, hollow candles and volume candles, with configurable rising and falling colours. Hollow and volume candles keep independent body, border and wick visibility and rising/falling colours, which persist in layouts and undo; series settings show and change the hollow and volume candle colours, and layouts saved before hollow or volume candles had these switches restore as they were drawn; volume candles and OHLC bars can also compare each close with the previous close. Added candle series accept a border width and per-bar body, wick and border colours; borders stay inside the body and default to disabled, on added volume candles too, while added hollow candles keep a 1.5 point border and take a positive width. The main series' last price line, its label and `series_last_value` of columns, hollow candles, and candles, volume candles and OHLC bars coloured by the previous close, take the direction of the last candle; added series compare the last bar's close with its open. Hollow and volume candles also take a bar's own body, border and wick colours. Candles, OHLC bars and volume candles that close at their open use rising colours, matching their last price line and label; volume bars, their tags and hover readout borders use the same direction rule.
- [x] Line, line with markers, step line, area, HLC area, baseline, columns and high-low charts; line and area charts accept a `PriceSource`, and the Columns chart keeps its own for the price each column reaches. High-low charts draw every bar in one colour and print each bar's high and low once zoomed in far enough to fit them, with independent label visibility, colour and size that persist in layouts and undo, and labels in the price scale's text colour until a colour is chosen and at 1 to 256 points; layouts and chart settings templates saved before these controls restore without labels. Added high-low series tag their last value and draw their price line in their bar colour. Added column series apply their configured hit tolerance around their painted bounds, including flat bodies, dense bars and single-point histories; zero uses the body bounds and all hits stay inside the plot. Baseline fills accept independent colours at the pane edge and the base on each price side, including point overrides, while older settings keep flat fills. Area and baseline plots support point markers with independent radii for main and added series. Added series retain their own curved and patterned lines, OHLC open-tick visibility and thin bars, area gradient endpoints, relative gradients and fill inversion. Line-based added series pulse continuously or on last-bar updates on their own pane and scale; expired update pulses stop requesting repaints.
- [x] Main-series conflation has a configurable physical-pixel threshold or can be disabled. `Chart::prepare_conflation` prepares the shared render cache before a dense view; groups retain price extremes, gaps and per-bar colours and refresh after data, price-source or grouping changes.
- [x] Heikin-Ashi, Renko, Kagi, line break, point and figure, and range-bar transformations and rendering. Heikin-Ashi retains independent body, border and wick visibility and rising/falling colours; Symbol settings also select original-only scale prices or the default pair of original and transformed closes, and series settings show and change the same colours. These choices persist in layouts and undo; layouts saved before them restore with the original close alone.
- [x] Renko box sizing and wicks, line-break lookback, and price-based chart parameters are configurable.
- [x] Market Profile (TPO) uses exchange sessions or calendar days, with letters in their blocks by default or plain blocks, initial balance, point of control, value area and composite profiles; autoscale fits the full displayed distributions and cropped profiles retain their session and central-level readouts. POC, value-area and optional midpoint captions keep a gutter at the first or offscreen session opening, separate nearby levels and reveal their prices on hover.
- [x] Volume Footprint and Session Volume Profile aggregate supplied executions on time, tick, volume, range and delta intervals with an instrument tick size; bars without complete retained executions use candles. Session profiles require `studies`, divide the chart by the chart's hours, the instrument's regular or extended hours, or a daily window of their own in any zone, and group executions into a row count or whole ticks per row; autoscale includes their full price rows after range edits, and manual crops retain session and POC/value-area readouts. Hosts can restrict the chart type menu and favourite strip to declared historical or live executions for the resolved instrument; unavailable favourites stay saved.
- [ ] Leave bars outside the chosen session hours out of session volume profiles instead of joining them to the session before.
- [x] Kagi lines change thickness at the shoulder or waist they cross within a continuing leg.
- [x] Point and figure charts take a high/low or close-only source from chart parameters and settings.
- [x] Range charts build their bars from the supplied executions, falling back to the OHLC approximation while the chart holds none; `Chart::range_bar_source` reports which one is shown.
- [x] Volume candle bodies scale with their share of the largest visible volume, narrowing to the wick width at zero volume.
- [x] HLC area charts draw high, low and close lines with their own colour, width and style, and two fills with their own colour and opacity, which a palette colour keeps; high line, low line and each fill can be hidden. The settings persist in layouts and undo, and layouts and chart templates saved with the former fill opacity and close line width restore as they were drawn. Kagi stroke widths are exposed in the settings.
- [x] TPO profiles can show a statistics table below them with the point of control, value area, initial balance range, TPO counts below and above the point of control, rotation factor and range extension, switched by one setting on the chart type and the study.
- [x] TPO single-print highlights and the midpoint caption and marker are off by default and switch on from the chart's settings or the study's inputs. Charts and studies saved before the midpoint had a setting restore with it shown.

### Pan, Zoom and Cursor

- [x] Cursor-centred wheel zoom, horizontal pan, drag pan, touch pan and pinch zoom. Plain dragging remains available after a drawing selection is released or its pane is removed. Rolling the wheel away zooms in on time and price axes; rolling it toward zooms out, including Ctrl/Command wheel and trackpad scrolling, while axis drags, Shift+wheel pan and pinches retain their directions. Scroll and scale gesture groups can be disabled independently without changing their individual permissions; disabling scrolling stops its momentum, and the scale switch also covers axis drags and resets in every pane.
- [x] Touch drags can start kinetic scrolling with configurable damping.
- [x] Axis drag scaling, double-click reset, box zoom and optional chart-owned navigation buttons. Fit content includes every loaded bar series across panes, including hidden series and whitespace, reserves the configured bar or pixel right margin, and respects spacing limits. An empty main series uses the union of added-series timestamps for its horizontal mapping. Live updates preserve the view on valued bars; filling a whitespace tail in its own slot follows only when both shift options are enabled and the view is near the live edge. Removing a whitespace tail through a pop, data replacement or replay seek keeps the right offset and any pending start index. Appended bars retain their new-bar follow policy. Bars arriving between frames, older or newer and in any number, on a chart type that rebuilds its bars or on a chart without main bars, keep a following view at its live edge, including with a fixed right edge, and a scrolled-back view on its bars.
- [x] Programmatic crosshair positions target a bar series in its own price units, scale and pane, with labels in their target axis columns on either side, pointer precedence and matching image exports. A hidden target keeps the time guide in every pane and restores its saved price readout when shown. Invalid positions and removed targets clear the crosshair. Positions set from code persist inside layouts with crosshair synchronization on or off, and return after a linked pointer leaves.
- [x] Cross, dot and arrow cursor modes, styled crosshair lines, price/time labels and OHLC snapping. One-point dots in axis-aligned lines and dotted construction guides cover at least one device pixel in each direction, including below one pixel per point. Both lines and both axis labels have independent visibility switches; saved options without label switches inherit each label's line switch; hidden mode suppresses every crosshair element. Independent time-scale filters exclude whitespace and hidden-only values from crosshair indices without removing stored data; visible series and study values keep their slots eligible. Line-based main and added series accept independent crosshair marker radii, fills, border colours and border widths, including on independent scales and in separate panes. Markers start hidden; enabling them without colour overrides keeps the point's line colour and a ring in the background at the marker or the secondary pane's fill. Added-series crosshair markers avoid allocating chart parameters on hovered frames.
- [x] Main-pane crosshair snapping includes visible studies and added series on their own scales for time-based charts. Close-only snapping compares visible main and added-series closes on every chart type, preferring the main series and then added-series draw order on equal screen distances.
- [x] Hover readouts and the data window show bar and study values. Touch holds activate after a configurable delay, move the readout without panning, and exit on release or retain their position until the next tap, independently of live-edge following. An activated readout owns its contact through release, so it neither clicks series nor counts as manual navigation. Holds work without keyboard focus, window focus loss cancels them, and resizing clears a latched position outside the plot. A latched price button keeps its value through a tap and returns the price action to the host. Crosshair notifications report the held or latched readout, including its activation delay and exit.
- [x] Mouse drags can start kinetic scrolling through their own option, independently of touch drags.
- [x] The box-zoom overlay uses the theme's information colour with a translucent fill.
- [x] The floating, held and tracking readouts lead with the instrument's symbol.
- [x] `Chart::data_table` and `DataTableDialog` show the loaded bars and visible study plots in a table, opened from the chart menu.

### Scales and Panes

- [x] Linear, logarithmic, percentage, indexed-to-100 and inverted price scales, autoscale, manual ranges and scale options. Secondary-pane crosshair labels use their axis units on relative scales, including shared studies and explicit percentage formatters. Series autoscale providers and attached primitives can reserve independent top and bottom logical-pixel margins, combined by edge maximum within each pane and scale and added to fractional margins after resizing; manual ranges ignore them, studies without values in view add no fallback prices while attached primitives still contribute, a scale with no price to fit keeps its range without reapplying pixel margins, and a pane too short for a request keeps its ordinary fit, including the room for markers. Main and added series independently style their 0% or indexed-100 reference lines with colour, width, pattern and visibility; saved styles retain these settings. Marker autoscaling reserves room for shapes, outlines and labels of visible markers positioned from bars or prices at their configured size on main and secondary panes; hidden markers and disabled autoscaling add no room.
- [x] Time-axis tick hierarchy, price marks that default to the instrument tick grid with host overrides, exchange time zones, session breaks and extended-hours shading.
- [x] Linear numeric horizontal scales for strikes and maturities use actual sample keys without placeholder bars; series, panes, ticks and cursor conversions share their positions. Time charts retain ordinal spacing. Custom horizontal domains accept categorical and structured host keys ordered by their type, with shared formatting, typed lookup and annotation placement; unknown keys are rejected and repeated keys update one slot. Drawing anchors and their saved coordinates follow keys on custom and numeric axes, including whitespace, regardless of the chart timeframe. Numeric and custom-key domains retain their initial spacing unit across updates, preserving pixel distances per key and the keys in a scrolled-back view, including historical insertions and added-series domains. Numeric bar bodies are capped by the closest neighbouring keys in view at the configured candle-width ratio, for main and added series. Volume and automatic study bars also fit these gaps, keeping their own width ratios.
- [x] Chart-wide time, price, percentage and volume formatters, plus per-series price formatters. Saved series styles declare price, abbreviated volume or raw percentage display with precision and minimum movement, independently of scale transforms. Their prices retain the chart's thousands grouping and their changes retain built-in signs; host formatter text stays as supplied. Logarithmic volume ticks retain their lower decades without changing linear abbreviated-unit spacing. Explicit series and chart price formatters take precedence; a shared axis takes tick settings from its first visible series, and each series retains its own tags and readouts. Price and percentage tick hooks receive the complete ordered list for each axis in its displayed units, including edge ticks; main and independent series axes measure their width from that same list, while crosshair and price tags keep individual-value formatting. Time tick hooks receive the current locale and display-time context and can defer individual ticks to the normal locale-aware text without changing crosshair formatting. Drawing prices and price changes use their pane's raw-price formatter, including measurements, forecasts, projections, position levels and rectangle heights. Position axis tags in separate series panes follow the owning series' formatter and display grid.
- [x] Locale-aware date names and configurable date patterns. Forecast dates use the chart's display time zone and crosshair time formatter, including host formatting and clock visibility.
- [x] Chart-owned panes share the time axis and support resizing, reordering, stretch sizing, collapse and maximise controls. Ctrl/Command plus double-click collapses or restores the pointed pane, including its header, and preserves sizing, focus, undo and saved layouts; outside every pane it stays an ordinary double-click. With the price pane collapsed, the other panes fill the chart and keep dragging, scrolling and pinching the time scale. Removing any pane, including the original price pane, deletes its members and annotations while preserving surviving panes and their horizontal mapping; the final pane is kept. Pane menus disable removal and merging when a contained study disallows user editing, and their member controls keep read-only studies in place, including on the price pane. Host pane operations remain available. Snapshots preserve layouts without the original pane; the main series stays out of such a layout while its bars keep arriving from the host or the feed for the studies and the time axis.
- [x] Main and added series and studies can move between panes. The main series retains its data, horizontal mapping, price scale, annotations and pointer readouts in its destination; snapshots and undo preserve placement. Studies and added series can use separate panes and independent scales. Named left, right, additional and hidden overlay assignments share options and ranges only within their pane; painting, axis controls, hit tests and coordinate queries follow those assignments after pane moves, and snapshots retain each pane's named scales. The demo menu's Samples entries open synthetic strike and maturity charts with numeric crosshair readouts and preserve the price workspace on return.
- [x] Pane-local axis enumeration and commands place every labelled scale at either edge or join their sources onto one scale while retaining raw values and series formatting. Unused axes hide by default and return when a source becomes visible; the policy and layout survive saving and undo. Axis and plot menus expose these controls in the library and demo, and chart settings edit the main series' pane.
- [x] Scale settings control currency and unit labels, scale-mode and price-action buttons, price-to-bar ratio, label overlap and symbol, study and extended-session value colours. The demo menu converts synthetic USD prices to US cents through the host feed contract, including history, executions and book prices, and keeps the unit through replay and saved layouts.
- [ ] Maximise the pane holding the main series by double-click, as study panes do.
- [ ] Study moves into new panes still append at the bottom; directional creation immediately above or below the current pane remains incomplete.
- [x] The Scale Price Chart Only setting keeps overlay studies and their primitives out of the main price scale's autoscale range, and the left-axis scale mode applies when the main scale is drawn on the left axis alone.
- [x] Added-series value tags and last-value queries use configured scale precision when no formatter is supplied, otherwise the nearest tick label's decimals with at least two; saved numeric display declarations also control their tags and readouts.
- [ ] Draw symbol-name and study-name tags on the price scale while their value tags are hidden; the names otherwise appear only inside shown value tags.
- [x] Scale font size, axis line colour and pane-button visibility settings apply to the price, study and time axes and the pane controls.

## Technical Indicators

### Calculations and Rendering

- [x] `IndicatorFactory` registers 204 indicators with default features and accepts downstream registrations; the study picker groups its built-in results on Indicators, Profiles, Patterns and Order flow pages, each offered only while it has an accepted study. Hosts can restrict Order flow entries, including search and favourites, to the feed services each study's inputs read; requirements are cached when feed metadata or the factory changes. Other categories keep their existing availability. The chooser has a 200-point sidebar, a fixed name header and favourite stars on hovered, focused or starred rows whose focus follows the study through reordering and whose labels and tooltips name the translated study; its light preset uses separate muted inks for section labels, the header and search hints.
- [x] Host-backed options profiles offer their help article in the legend and study menu and appear on Profiles for declared option-chain feeds, with nearest or listed expiries and inclusive date cutoffs, separate volume and open-interest measures, split or net sides, put/call readouts and an optional multiplier-weighted max-pain level. Missing contract fields hide the inputs that need them; saved measures and levels wait until those fields arrive. Saved profiles restore expiry and measure by label while keeping explicit editor changes and resets, with Nearest for an expiry no longer listed. Gamma exposure uses the selected chain rows or the feed's supplied snapshot, with signed net bars from zero by default, separate call and put bars, optional call and put walls that omit sides with no exposure, and zero gamma only from the provider. Legends and strike hover show units, the sign convention and snapshot time; every snapshot replaces the bars and levels. Expected move is off by default and draws a labelled expiry band from the nearest-strike straddle mids or ATM implied volatility with calendar-day scaling; missing inputs and expired dates remove the band. Historical chains use their requested observation instant and the latest fallback close known then, using the next bar opening as its close boundary and the final bar from its own opening. Hidden profiles retain their computed values for cursor payloads and alerts while omitting their chart geometry.
- [x] Moving-average families include SMA, EMA, WMA, HMA, ALMA, KAMA and configurable combinations, ribbons and crossing markers.
- [x] Momentum and trend studies include RSI, MACD, Stochastic, ADX, SuperTrend, Ichimoku and Parabolic SAR. RSI opens with a middle level and an RSI-based moving average. RSI, Williams %R and Rank Correlation offer the same smoothing inputs: a type of none, SMA, SMA with Bollinger bands, EMA, SMMA (RMA), WMA or VWMA, a length, and a band deviation that shows only for the band type.
- [x] Volatility and channel studies include Bollinger Bands, ATR, Keltner, Donchian and historical volatility. Bollinger Bands, Envelopes and Keltner expose source and band inputs, independent plot value lines and a band visibility switch that retains its colour and opacity even when boundary plots are hidden. Preview, cancellation, history and saved templates retain these settings; Keltner source edits change its basis while its range calculations keep the original bars. Envelopes and Keltner select an exponential basis with a checkbox, off and on respectively by default; older saved average choices still load. Donchian fills the area between its outer bands with a faint blue by default; applied settings retain the band colour, opacity and visibility separately from its plot styles and latest-value lines.
- [x] Volume studies include OBV, MFI, accumulation/distribution and VWAP; VWAP supports calendar/session and supplied event anchors with deviation or percentage bands, and opens with one filled band at one standard deviation.
- [x] Visible, fixed, session, periodic, anchored and auto-anchored volume profiles sum supplied executions in whole-tick rows, mark incomplete spans and fall back to finer-bar or chart-bar estimates, with translucent cyan and pink rows that keep candles readable and stay stronger in the value area, session and periodic rows sized by a Width input of 30% of their span by default, and a 2 px point of control in the foreground colour across each profile's span, or across the whole plot for the visible range. ## Provided by the host

The crate renders supplied data and exposes interfaces for application services. The application supplies market data, broker execution, accounts and durable storage. These interfaces also allow services to run remotely.

- `Datafeed`, `SymbolSearch`, `Quotes` and `Marks` connect instrument discovery, history, live prices, executions, depth and chart events. Demo marks follow newly streamed and older paged synthetic bars with stable symbol/time identities. Feed queries also supply metrics, options volume, listed option expiries and live or historical option chains with quotes, open interest, implied volatility and Greeks, analyst targets, symbol details, news, calendars, screeners and hotlists. The host supplies market heatmap universes and metrics, and the terminal updates their prices and changes from `Quotes`. The headless `OptionChain` handle retains the latest validated snapshot and error; the host supplies all option data. `OptionExposure::gamma` computes exposure per 1% underlying move by expiry and strike, counting contracts with missing inputs and using positive call and negative put signs as a convention. `Datafeed::option_exposure` supplies gamma, delta, vanna or charm snapshots with their units and model label; `OptionExposureHandle` retains the latest valid observation and prefers it unchanged over computed gamma.
- `Broker` supplies real or paper-account state, trading rules, execution and account updates. Trading components submit actions and render the reported orders, positions, fills and history.
- `Storage` supplies layouts, drawings, settings and template documents; an application may use local or remote storage and manage accounts, synchronisation and sharing.
- `Alerts` stores and evaluates alert conditions and reports changes and triggers. The crate edits models, including the notification schedules a service applies, and draws levels, dialogs, logs and toasts; the application supplies sound and external delivery.
- `Signals` supplies markers, levels and zones with category visibility and click callbacks. Captions yield to price bars, signal borders and other captions, prioritising recent markers; hover retains the labels and details of coincident events. Applications can deliver iceberg, absorption, spoofing, large-order and other interpreted order-flow events through it.
- `Notifications` and `Chats` supply notification records and conversation messages. Publishing and sharing actions are application intents; `Posts` resolves the links of post and idea drawings through `Chart::set_post_source`, and the chart never fetches a link itself.
- `StrategyReport` supplies the trade list, equity and performance inputs for the report panel; `CompanyFinancials` supplies financial tables. Applications also provide translations, additional fonts, image loaders and platform save/clipboard facilities.
